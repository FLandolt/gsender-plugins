Plugins

Each plugin is a single zip. Try it in your browser first — in the demo, Load to gSender downloads the G-code instead.

Always check before you cut. Look at the toolpath preview, run the job in the air first, and keep a hand on the stop button. You use these plugins at your own risk.

How to use a plugin

Three steps, no accounts.

  1. Download

    Grab the zip of the plugin you want from the cards above.

  2. Add it to gSender

    Unzip it into gSender's plugins folder so you get plugins/<name>/gsender-plugin.json, then restart gSender.

  3. Open and cut

    Open the plugin from gSender's tools page, enter your numbers, check the 3D preview and press Load to gSender.
