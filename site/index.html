Plugins

Pick a tool. Download it. Cut.

Each plugin is a single zip. Try it in your browser first — in the demo, Load to gSender downloads the G-code instead.

Always check before you cut. Look at the preview, run the job in the air first, and keep a hand on the stop button. You use these plugins at your own risk.

How it works

Three steps, no accounts.

Plugins live in gSender's own plugins folder.

01

Download

Grab the zip of the plugin you want from the cards above.

02

Add it to gSender

Unzip it into gSender's plugins folder so you get plugins/<name>/gsender-plugin.json, then restart gSender.

03

Open and cut

Open the plugin from gSender's tools page, enter your numbers, check the preview and press Load to gSender.
